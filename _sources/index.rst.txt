   <!-- ── Why Twiga ─────────────────────────────────────────── -->
   <div class="tw-why">
     <p class="tw-why-lead">
       Most forecasting libraries force a choice between ML and deep learning, between
       point forecasts and probabilistic ones, or between flexibility and production
       readiness. Twiga removes that trade-off.
     </p>

     <div class="tw-feature-grid">

       <div class="tw-fcard">
         <div class="tw-fcard-header">
           <span class="tw-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#0f718e,#00bfa5)">
             <i class="bi bi-arrow-left-right"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="tw-fcard-title">One interface, every model</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="tw-fcard-body">A single <code>TwigaForecaster</code> trains, tunes,
         evaluates and backtests any model through an identical API. Switching from a
         gradient-boosted tree to a neural network means changing one config object,
         not rewriting code.</p>
         <a href="models/index.html" class="tw-fcard-link">
           Explore models <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i>
         </a>
       </div>

       <div class="tw-fcard">
         <div class="tw-fcard-header">
           <span class="tw-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#6a1b9a,#8e24aa)">
             <i class="bi bi-layers-fill"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="tw-fcard-title">Composable probabilistic architecture</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="tw-fcard-body">Any backbone pairs with any distribution head without
         modification, giving a full matrix of probabilistic models from a small set of
         reusable building blocks. Adding a new architecture or head is a focused,
         isolated change.</p>
         <a href="probabilistic/index.html" class="tw-fcard-link">
           Explore probabilistic <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i>
         </a>
       </div>

       <div class="tw-fcard">
         <div class="tw-fcard-header">
           <span class="tw-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#2e7d32,#43a047)">
             <i class="bi bi-shield-check"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="tw-fcard-title">Calibrated uncertainty on any model</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="tw-fcard-body">Conformal prediction wraps any trained model, including
         plain point-forecast models like LightGBM, with finite-sample intervals that
         carry a formal coverage guarantee. One call after training, no retraining and
         no distributional assumptions required.</p>
         <a href="probabilistic/conformal-prediction.html" class="tw-fcard-link">
           Explore conformal prediction <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i>
         </a>
       </div>

       <div class="tw-fcard">
         <div class="tw-fcard-header">
           <span class="tw-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#e65100,#fb8c00)">
             <i class="bi bi-sliders"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="tw-fcard-title">Experiment-ready by default</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="tw-fcard-body">Every component is configured through validated Pydantic
         dataclasses that are serialisable and directly wired to Optuna search spaces.
         Hyperparameter tuning, time-based backtesting and checkpoint-based resume are
         first-class features.</p>
         <a href="core/index.html" class="tw-fcard-link">
           Explore handbook <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i>
         </a>
       </div>

       <div class="tw-fcard">
         <div class="tw-fcard-header">
           <span class="tw-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#00695c,#00897b)">
             <i class="bi bi-graph-up-arrow"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="tw-fcard-title">Statistical analysis and feature intelligence</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="tw-fcard-body">Eight feature-target association measures are computed
         in a single call and fused with Borda-count rank aggregation and random-forest
         importance to produce a robust ranked feature list. Stationarity tests, entropy
         measures and residual diagnostics are included, all returning tidy DataFrames.</p>
         <a href="core/feature-analysis.html" class="tw-fcard-link">
           Explore feature analysis <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i>
         </a>
       </div>

       <div class="tw-fcard">
         <div class="tw-fcard-header">
           <span class="tw-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#880e4f,#c2185b)">
             <i class="bi bi-gear-wide-connected"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="tw-fcard-title">Production MLOps out of the box</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="tw-fcard-body">An optional five-module stack covers experiment
         tracking, versioned checkpoints, drift monitoring, a production REST API and
         automated retraining as a schedulable workflow. All modules install together
         and operate without external infrastructure.</p>
         <a href="mlops/index.html" class="tw-fcard-link">
           Explore MLOps <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i>
         </a>
       </div>

     </div>
   </div>
